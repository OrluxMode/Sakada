One delivery, three people, no guesswork about what happens next.

A farmer or vendor books a delivery. A driver accepts it. Everyone sees the same status, updated in real time, until the goods arrive.

If you're sending goods (farmer or vendor)

  1. 1

    Create the delivery

    Enter your pickup point, destination, what you're moving, and roughly when it needs to go.

  2. 2

    Get an estimated cost

    Sakada calculates a fee estimate before you commit to anything.

  3. 3

    Confirm and wait for a driver

    Your request goes live to available drivers. You'll see "Driver Assigned" the moment one accepts.

  4. 4

    Track the trip

    Watch your delivery move through Picked Up, In Transit, and Delivered — no need to call and ask.

  5. 5

    Rate the service

    Once it's delivered, leave a rating and it's saved to your delivery history for next time.

If you're driving (driver)

  1. 1

    Browse available deliveries

    See pickup and drop-off points, what's being moved, and the estimated fee before you accept anything.

  2. 2

    Accept a delivery

    Once you accept, it's assigned to you and moves to your active deliveries list.

  3. 3

    Update status as you go

    Mark Picked Up, then In Transit, then Delivered — the farmer or vendor sees each update immediately.

  4. 4

    Get rated, build your history

    Completed deliveries and ratings stay on your profile.
