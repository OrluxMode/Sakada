See the fare before you book

Sakada works it out from the road distance and the weight, before you commit to anything. Here's a worked example.

₱150 base + ₱15 per km + ₱2 per kg

Example: 115 km, 500 kg

Base fare
₱150
115 km × ₱15
₱1,725
500 kg × ₱2
₱1,000
Estimated fare
₱2,875

Your estimate uses the road distance from the map, not a straight line.

The fare you book is the fare you pay. No haggling at drop-off.

For now, you pay the driver directly. In-app payment (GCash or cash on delivery) is coming soon.

How much can I send?

There's no fixed limit. Drivers see the weight before they accept, so they only take loads their truck can carry. As a guide, a small truck carries about 1–2 tonnes and a six-wheeler about 4–7 tonnes.
