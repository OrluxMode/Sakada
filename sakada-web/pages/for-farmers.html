Before you hand over your harvest

Straight answers to what farmers ask first. Something else? Contact us.

Who pays the driver?

Your buyer does, in cash, when the goods arrive. Like cash on delivery: you ship, they pay the fare for this trip, the same amount shown when you booked. If the driver asks for more, show the fare in your booking and contact us.

What if something goes wrong after a driver accepts?

Contact us with your booking code. Until your goods are picked up, we can cancel the booking for you.

What if no driver accepts?

Your booking stays Pending until one does. You get an in-app notification when it's accepted, and you can cancel it yourself any time before.

How heavy can my load be?

There's no fixed limit. Drivers see the weight before they accept, so they only take loads their truck can carry.
