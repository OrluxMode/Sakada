Before you take your first job

Straight answers to what drivers ask first. Something else? Contact us.

How do I get paid?

In cash, by the customer receiving the goods at drop-off: the fare shown on the job, tolls included. If anyone disputes it, the booked fare is in the booking, and you can contact us.

Can I choose which jobs I take?

Yes. Browse the available deliveries and accept only the ones that fit your route and your truck.

What do customers see about me?

Your name once you accept, plus your vehicle type and plate number if you add them on your dashboard.

What if another driver accepts first?

The job leaves the list. If you both tapped Accept at the same moment, you'll see that it was taken and can pick another.
