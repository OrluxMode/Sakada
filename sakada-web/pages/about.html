Built to fix a real coordination problem.

Right now, farmers and vendors arrange agricultural transport through a mix of suppliers, traders, trucking services, and personal contacts. It works, but it's slow, and there's no single place to see what's happening with a delivery. Sakada puts that whole process in one system.

Who it's for

Farmers

Create delivery requests, track outgoing goods, view delivery history, get updates, and rate the service.

Vendors

Create delivery requests, receive agricultural goods, track incoming deliveries, view history, and rate the service.

Drivers

View available deliveries, accept jobs, update delivery status, and build a record of completed work.
