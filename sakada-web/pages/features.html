Everything you need to move agricultural goods, in one place.

No separate calls to suppliers, traders, or trucking contacts. Sakada handles booking, matching, tracking, and history for every delivery.

Core features

Registration & login

Separate accounts for farmers, vendors, and drivers, each with their own dashboard.

Delivery booking

Enter pickup, destination, goods, and schedule to create a request in minutes.

Scheduling

Set when a pickup needs to happen, not just where.

Provider matching

Available drivers see your request and accept the ones that fit their route.

Cost estimation

See an estimated fee before you confirm a booking.

Real-time tracking

Follow a delivery's location while it's in transit.

Status updates

Pending, Driver Assigned, Picked Up, In Transit, Delivered — updated as it happens.

Notifications

Get told when a driver accepts, picks up, or delivers your goods.

Delivery history

Every completed delivery stays on record for farmers, vendors, and drivers alike.

Ratings & feedback

Rate the service after each delivery, building trust for future bookings.
