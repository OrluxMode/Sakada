Know the fare before you book

Sakada works it out from the road distance and the weight. Here's an example: onions from a farm in Nueva Ecija to your stall at the Marikina palengke.

No haggling at drop-off

The fare is locked in when you book. It already includes expressway tolls, and the driver is paid exactly that amount.

Measured along the road

The distance comes from the map's driving route, not a straight line, so the fare matches the trip the truck makes.

Before you book your next delivery

Straight answers to what vendors ask first. Something else? Contact us.

Can I book a pickup from my supplier?

Yes. Set the pickup at your farmer or supplier and the drop-off at your stall or store. You book it and pay the driver; your supplier just hands over the goods.

When and how do I pay?

In cash to the driver when the goods reach you: the fare for this trip, the same amount you saw when you booked. If the driver asks for more, show them the fare in your booking and contact us.

What if no driver accepts?

Your booking stays Pending until one does. You get an in-app notification when it's accepted, and you can cancel it yourself any time before.

What if something goes wrong after a driver accepts?

Contact us with your booking code. Until the goods are picked up, we can cancel the booking for you. Every past delivery stays in your history with its fare.
