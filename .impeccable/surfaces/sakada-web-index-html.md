# Landing page (sakada-web/index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Audience on desktop: business owners and vendors, indoors. On mobile: vendors and owners, mixed conditions. Job: understand that one booking replaces calling around, see that the fare is fair and computed, then book or sign up in their role.
Proof on hand: fare formula ₱150 + ₱15/km + ₱2/kg, five live delivery stages, three roles, nationwide coverage. GCash or COD payment is planned and must be labeled as coming. No testimonials, counts, or partner logos exist; do not invent them.
Status: two options are in Figma for the owner to judge. Option A refines the incumbent dark/amber world. Option B is this direction. Unresolved: which option ships, and copy language (English for now; Taglish was offered).

## Direction contract
THESIS: The page is a printed produce sack at the bagsakan, and the fare is a cardboard price placard taped to it. This refuses the category default of a dark truck photo with a gradient, a headline, a CTA, and a stat row.
OWN-WORLD: Woven polypropylene sack-white ground with a visible weave. Stencil red ink for display and the primary action, and sack-band green for structure bands and nav. Kraft cardboard placards with black marker numerals. Stitched dashed seams divide regions. Stenciled tag boxes act as role buttons. Big Shoulders Stencil for display, Permanent Marker only for prices, Archivo for body.
STORY: A visitor reads the stencil promise, sees a real fare worked out on the placard (₱150 + km × ₱15 + kg × ₱2), books it on the sewn-on label form, or picks a role tag to sign up. Below: how the fare is computed, one unbroken five-stage tracking row with the current stage lit, roles, and payment (coming).
FIRST VIEWPORT: A green sack band holds the nav (stenciled SAKADA, links, Log In, red Sign up). Left 58%: 3-line red stencil headline at about 120px, an Archivo lede, and role tags. Right: a sewn-on label holding a working quote form (pickup, drop-off, kg), overlapped by a placard tilted −3° showing a sample fare in marker, with the red "Book this delivery" button. A bottom seam and green band carry the stage row.
FORM: Bagsakan sack-stencil + market price placard, #3 on the grounded list (1 carbon waybill, 2 DPWH km posts, 3 sack + placard, 4 jeepney sign-painting, 5 timbangan, 6 tarpaulin, 7 banig). Seed 0783fa8c. Raises: stage row is one unbroken timeline that always shows where "now" is (drum-machine step row). Fare numbers use tabular numerals with the working shown (forge measurements). Quote recalculates as inputs change (algorave: change visible before it lands). The chosen role tag pulls proud of the row (cereal aisle). Competitive alternate: airport-yellow wayfinding (wins on clarity, loses on audience identification).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
